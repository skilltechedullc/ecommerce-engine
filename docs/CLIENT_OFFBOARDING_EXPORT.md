# Client Offboarding And Export Checklist

Use this if a client wants to leave, move providers, or receive a full data copy.

## Data To Export

- Products.
- Product variants.
- Product images.
- Orders.
- Order items.
- Shipments.
- Notification logs.
- Audit logs.
- Store settings drafts and published snapshots.
- Uploaded product media.

## Account Ownership

- Confirm the client controls Supabase.
- Confirm the client controls hosting.
- Confirm the client controls domain/DNS.
- Confirm the client controls Razorpay/payment account.
- Confirm the client controls email and WhatsApp provider accounts.

## Handover

- Provide latest deployment branch/tag.
- Provide migration list.
- Provide env variable list without exposing unrelated client secrets.
- Provide backup timestamp and storage location.
- Disable maintenance access if support contract ends.
- Confirm final support window and contact route.
