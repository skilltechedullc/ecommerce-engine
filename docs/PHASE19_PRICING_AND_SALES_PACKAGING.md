# Phase 19 Pricing And Sales Packaging

Status: draft pricing and package positioning are defined for India and UAE/international clients.

## India Setup Pricing

- Basic Store: INR 15,000 - 35,000.
- Business Store: INR 40,000 - 85,000.
- Premium Store: INR 90,000 - 250,000+.

## UAE Setup Pricing

- Basic Store: AED 1,500 - 3,500.
- Business Store: AED 4,000 - 8,000.
- Premium Store: AED 9,000 - 25,000+.

## Monthly Maintenance

- India basic support: INR 2,000 - 5,000/month.
- India managed support: INR 7,000 - 20,000/month.
- UAE basic support: AED 300 - 800/month.
- UAE managed support: AED 1,000 - 3,000/month.

## Package Positioning

- Basic Store: ecommerce website, product catalog, cart, checkout, admin panel, order management, basic branding, payment setup, and training.
- Business Store: Basic Store plus WhatsApp order support, product import, categories, coupons/offers, customer order tracking, email notifications, delivery/shipping settings, and improved homepage customization.
- Premium Store: Business Store plus WhatsApp automation, AI assistant, advanced analytics, multi-language, multi-currency, delivery zones/time slots, courier integration, loyalty/referral, and custom design.

## Add-On Pricing Notes

- Price WhatsApp automation, AI assistant, shipping integration, multi-language, multi-currency, product upload/import, advanced analytics, custom design, and maintenance as separate add-ons when they are outside the purchased package.
- For grocery/supermarket clients, sell the build as an operations system rather than a cheap brochure website.
- Maintenance should define included small changes, response time, backup checks, update cadence, and paid change request boundaries.
